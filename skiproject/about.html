About Us

We are a group of friends from Montreal who love to ski. Because we love tackling the whole mountain, our gear bags used to be packed with multiple pairs of skis. We often found ourselves gliding down the mountain, wanting to do a bit of everything—shredding the terrain park, dropping off-trail, or carving through fresh powder. However, we kept running into the same frustrating problem: we either had to head back to the lodge to swap our skis, or accept a drop in performance because our current gear wasn’t built for the changing snow conditions. We knew there had to be a better way. That is why we founded Slope Style. Our mission was to engineer a single, high-performance ski that truly does it all. Whether you are hitting features in the park, exploring technical off-trail lines, or floating through deep powder, Slope Style adapts instantly so you can ski at your absolute potential.
