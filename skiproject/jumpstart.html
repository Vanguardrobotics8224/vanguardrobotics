JUMPSTART

We donate 10% every year to the Jumpstart Fondation. Canadian Tire Jumpstart Charities is a Canadian charity committed to ensuring that every child has the opportunity to experience the life-changing benefits of organized sport and physical activity. Recognizing that financial barriers should never stand in the way of play, the foundation provides funding to families in financial need to help cover costs associated with registration, equipment, and transportation.Beyond individual grants, Jumpstart invests in large-scale infrastructure projects, such as the Jumpstart Inclusive Play Project, which creates accessible playgrounds and multi-sport courts where children of all abilities can play side-by-side. Since its inception in 2005, the foundation has helped more than 3 million kids across Canada get off the sidelines and into the game, fostering a generation that is healthier, more confident, and more connected to their community.
