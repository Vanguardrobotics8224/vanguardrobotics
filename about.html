About Us

We're a group of friends from Montreal that love to ski. We each have multiple different types of skis. When we were gliding on the mounting, and we wanted to do a bit of everything whether it be off trail, snow park or even powder we were getting frustrated that we either have to change skis or just not be at our best potential since we don't have the best ski for the conditions. We decided to found this Slope Style, a ski that can do everything. Whether it be park, off trail or even powder you will be at your full potential.
